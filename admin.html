📝 Create a Google Form

Paste the link of any mapping Google Sheet (with District, Assembly, Block columns) — a cascading form (District → Assembly → Block → Name → Mobile) will be created automatically, and its link will appear right here

The first time it opens, Google will ask you to authorize (using your own Google account) — the form will be created in the Drive of whoever authorizes it at that time.
